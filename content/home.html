What a CIO partner actually does

Six things most companies need and few have an owner for. Pick one. Most clients start with the bill or the baseline.

1

CIO-as-a-Service

A fractional or interim CIO with a fixed monthly scope: the plan, the vendors, the security program and the board page, run by someone who holds the same job elsewhere.

2

Security Sprint & Cyber Baseline

Thirty days to a plain-language baseline: what is exposed, what is covered, what to fix first, and what to tell your insurer and your board.

3

Bill Reconciliation & Sourcing

Telecom, network, phones and mobile, rebuilt line by line, then competed. The contract is yours; the savings are yours.

4

Acquisition Integration (RSI Decipher™)

Identity, tenants, email and access merged without a bad day for the people you just bought. A method built on 25 acquisitions, not a template.

5

Vendor Evaluation & Oversight

A real competition with real requirements, a scorecard you can defend, and someone who stays to make the winner deliver.

6

Board & CFO Technology Plan

One page your CFO can read in five minutes: where the money goes, what the risks are, and the three decisions that matter this year.

How it works

The same four steps whether you run 20 locations or 600. No step requires you to buy anything from us.

1

Listen

A 20-minute Technology Clarity Call. You bring the one decision on your desk. You leave with what we would do next.

2

Baseline

Invoices, contracts, vendors, security. Reconciled and written down in plain English. Usually ten business days.

3

Fix and compete

Contracts competed, risks closed, vendors held to the scorecard. Your name on every agreement.

4

Stay in the room

Monthly, with a fixed scope and a one-page report. We leave when it runs without us, and we say so.
